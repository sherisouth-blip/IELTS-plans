What happened next?

Many people did ask questions. A security camera showed police officers taking Kian away a short time before the shots. Witnesses told the court what they saw.

In November 2018, a court found three police officers guilty of murder. The court did not believe the police story. It was the first time police officers were found guilty of a killing in the drug war.

  1. How do we know what happened to Kian? Name two kinds of evidence.
  2. Most victims had no camera and no witnesses who would speak. What does that mean for their families?

The big idea

Every person and every country has a circle: the people they feel responsible for. People inside the circle are protected. When they are hurt, others want justice.

A label can push a person outside the circle. When people outside the circle are hurt, others say "they deserved it," or they say nothing.

That is what happened in Text A. Kian's name brought him back inside the circle, and then people wanted to know the truth.

Teachers call this circle the universe of obligation.

Back to the book: The official story called Jun "a drug user." What is Jay trying to do for Jun that Kian's family did for Kian?

Let's learn

Prejudice or discrimination?

They are different. Prejudice is a thought. Discrimination is an action.

Prejudice (a thought)

A shop guard thinks, "Teenagers always steal."

Discrimination (an action)

The shop guard follows only teenagers around the shop.

Prejudice can lead to discrimination. But you can have one without the other.
