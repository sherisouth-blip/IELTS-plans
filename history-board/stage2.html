Step 1: Key words

victim — a person who is hurt or killed by someone else.
The victims of the drug war were people killed by police or by unknown gunmen.
poor — having very little money.
Many poor families in Manila live in small, crowded houses.
evidence — facts that help show what is true.
A video can be evidence of what really happened.
prejudice — an unfair idea about a group of people, before you know them.
"All poor people are criminals" is prejudice.
discrimination — treating a group of people unfairly because of who they are.
Only arresting poor people, and not rich people, would be discrimination.

Step 2: Prejudice or discrimination?

They are different. Prejudice is a thought. Discrimination is an action.

Prejudice (a thought)

A shop guard thinks, "Teenagers always steal."

Discrimination (an action)

The shop guard follows only teenagers around the shop.

Prejudice can lead to discrimination. But you can have one without the other.
