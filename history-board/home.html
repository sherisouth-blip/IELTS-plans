History project · Patron Saints of Nothing

The Drug War in the Philippines

Compelling question: How can we know what really happened in the past?
Lessons Board Vocabulary

This project follows Jay's search for the truth in Patron Saints of Nothing. You will study the real history behind the book in six stages. Each lesson is complete on its own, and each one adds cards to your board.

Stage 1

Identity

Jay and me: what we share, the "hyphenated" dedication, insiders and outsiders, groups and labels.
Stage 2

We and they

Your circle, prejudice or discrimination, and four short sources about who died.
Stage 3

The history

Build the timeline of the drug war on your board, one source at a time.
Stage 4

Choices

Upstanders and bystanders: the journalists who worked at night, and the families who went to court.
Stage 5

Judgment, memory and legacy

A court for the whole world: the ICC, the trial, and why an accused person is presumed innocent.
Stage 6 Coming

Choosing to participate

Your 3-minute narrated documentary: your answer to the compelling question.
Supporting questions
  1. Who died in the drug war?
  2. What did the police say happened?
  3. What do the families say happened?
  4. Why were most of the victims poor?
  5. What evidence can show the truth?
