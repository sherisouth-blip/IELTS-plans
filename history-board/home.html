The Drug War in the Philippines

Compelling question: How can we know what really happened in the past?
Lessons Board Vocabulary

This project follows Jay's search for the truth in Patron Saints of Nothing. You will study the real history behind the book in six stages. Each lesson is complete on its own, and each one adds cards to your board.

Stage 1 Identity

Who is telling the story?

Jay and me, insiders and outsiders, groups and labels.
Adds to your answer: an insider and an outsider can see the same thing differently.
Stage 2 We and they

Same person, different label

Your circle, Kian's story, and sources about who died and why most were poor.
Adds to your answer: a label can change what people believe, and evidence can prove a label wrong.
Supporting questions 1 and 4
Stage 3 The history Lesson coming

Two stories of one night

For now: build the timeline of the drug war on your board, one source at a time.
Adds to your answer: police reports and families' stories can tell the same night in very different ways.
Supporting questions 2 and 3
Stage 4 Choices

Who records the truth?

Photojournalists, the nightcrawlers, and the families who went to court.
Adds to your answer: photos and records keep the truth from being forgotten, but they can't always prove who did it.
Supporting questions 3 and 5
Stage 5 Judgment, memory and legacy

Can a court decide what happened?

The ICC, the trial, and why an accused person is presumed innocent.
Adds to your answer: a trial tests the evidence from both sides before anyone is called guilty.
Supporting questions 2 and 5
